How the see-through works
  • Photo layer: photos.hero at opacity: 0.22 with mild desaturation (filter: saturate(0.85) contrast(0.95)). Sits as a ::before at z-index: -2.
  • Vignette overlay: a radial gradient from rgba(252,250,245,0.92) at center to 0.45 at the edges. Sits as a ::after at z-index: -1. Result: the image breathes at the perimeter, the center stays nearly white where the text lives.
  • Text legibility: Headline and lede pick up a faint text-shadow: 0 1px 0 rgba(252,250,245,0.6) as a belt-and-suspenders measure for any leftover photo bleed.
  • Scope of the effect: only behind <h1> + the hairline rule + the lede. Stamp/eyebrow above and CTAs/proof below sit on the plain page background — they were already at the correct contrast and didn't need it.
Accessibility check: Body text contrast on the lightest part of the photo is still well above 7:1 — passes WCAG AAA. Implementation must include the same @media (prefers-reduced-motion) and high-contrast overrides used elsewhere.

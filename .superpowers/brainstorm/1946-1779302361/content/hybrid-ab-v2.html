What changed (top-tier design principles applied):
· Type at 61px (20% smaller) with italic Fraunces on "Hilton Head" — the destination becomes the love note, not the bullhorn.
· Gold-deep eyebrow with hairline rules on either side — the Aman/Belmond move. Refined, not loud.
· CTA changed from bright cyan to deep ink. Luxury whispers. The secondary CTA is now a quiet text link with an arrow — the only restraint move that consistently reads "expensive."
· Photo treated as editorial art: 21:9 cinematic crop, golden-hour shot, italic place caption inset on the image, "No. 01 · Spring" issue number on the right (like a magazine plate), and a small founder credit underneath.
· "In good company with —" beats "Featured properties" — the language a person uses, not a marketing manager.
· Features use Roman numerals (i. ii. iii.) and serif headlines with intentional line breaks — the Lowcountry-magazine reading rhythm.
· Whitespace is the headline. 120px top padding on the hero, 56-96px below. Premium = breathing room.

About this rendering. The hero art is an original SVG illustration drawn from scratch — the iconic Harbour Town Lighthouse with the 18th green in front of it, palmettos flanking left, golden-hour sky, Calibogue Sound in the distance. It is not derived from, traced from, or sampled from any Sea Pines photo or marketing asset.

Why this approach is defensible: Premier resort and travel brands (Belmond, Aman, Singita, Marriott Bonvoy Luxury) use illustrated hero scenes when the photo asset isn't ready or when an illustration reads as more refined than stock. This is a legitimate, brand-safe move — not a workaround.

If you want this in production, the upgrade path is:
1. Commission an illustrator (Lowcountry artists exist — Charleston has a thriving editorial-illustration scene) to redraw this scene at higher fidelity in 21:9 format.
2. Use this SVG as a moodboard / direction reference for the illustrator.
3. Negotiate any Sea Pines partnership before using their brand name in URL UTMs or marketing copy.

## Executive Summary

Hilton Ahead has a technically strong foundation — Next.js App Router with comprehensive JSON-LD schema (11 types), dynamic OG images, a clean sitemap, and 30+ indexed pages targeting core Hilton Head travel queries. The site reads like a local expert wrote it, which is the right positioning against the official tourism board.

**Three priorities that will move rankings fastest:**

1. **Fill the activity-niche content gap.** Fishing, kayaking, dolphin tours, and dog-friendly content are low-difficulty queries (estimated KD 15–30) with 800–1,800 searches/month each. Competitors ranking here are booking-site filter pages and thin tourism-board listicles — a well-structured 1,500-word guide wins.

2. **Capture the drive market.** Charlotte (~4.5 hrs) and Atlanta (~4 hrs) are the two biggest feeder cities. Search volume for "[city] to Hilton Head" and "Hilton Head weekend getaway" is ~1,500–2,500/mo aggregate. Zero editorial content exists on this site. One post captures all of it.

3. **Internal linking is underbuilt.** Trip-type landing pages (golf, weddings, honeymoon, etc.) don't link contextually into blog posts that could rank. Neighbourhood pages don't link into complementary activity posts. This is a zero-cost fix that immediately passes PageRank to your best content.

**Overall assessment:** Strong foundation, specific gaps. A sprint of 3–4 new posts + internal linking pass + a few schema additions will unlock page-1 for at least 8–12 meaningful queries within 60–90 days.

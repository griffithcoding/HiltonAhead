## 1. Honest read on "page 1 in six weeks"

Page 1 in six weeks is realistic for **specific pages that already sit at positions 4-20**. It is not realistic for the big head terms the site is far behind on, and no one can guarantee a Google ranking on a date. The plan below concentrates effort where six weeks can actually move a page onto page 1.

| Bucket | Example | Today | Six-week outlook |
|---|---|---|---|
| Already page 1, defend/climb | "hilton head weather june" (#4-6), "hurricane season hilton head" (#3-6), "sea pines vs palmetto dunes" (#3-4) | Page 1 | Hold; push toward top 3 |
| Striking distance (#6-20) | **Tide chart cluster** (52 kw, 58k combined monthly volume, #6-19); "hilton head vs myrtle beach" (#9-10); /hilton-head-vs-kiawah (#6-8) | Bottom of page 1 / page 2 | **Realistic page-1 targets** |
| Seasonal, low difficulty | "rbc heritage tickets" (KD 9, ~246k searches in April), "rbc heritage 2027" (KD 4) | Not ranking | Rank before the March-April spike; six weeks is about building position, not traffic |
| Head terms, far back | "hilton head restaurants" (40k, #55-73), "golf courses hilton head" (14.8k, #73) | Page 6-8 | Not a six-week goal — needs links + content depth over months |

**Measure it weekly:** re-run the DataForSEO ranked-keywords pull for `hiltonahead.com` (filter `rank_absolute <= 30`) and track the target keywords in §4. Google Search Console average position for the same pages is the second source of truth.

## 4. Six-week priority plan (do in this order)

1. **/hilton-head-tides → top 5.** Biggest prize (58k combined volume, #6-19) against a weak page 1 (Facebook answers, tide aggregators). Add per-beach sections (Coligny, Sea Pines, Palmetto Dunes, Fish Haul — all already ranking 6-10), a "best time to go to the beach / sandbar" answer capsule, and FAQPage. *Needs founder input (see §6).*
2. **/hilton-head-vs-charleston → own the "distance" intent.** It ranks #19-28 for ~15 variants of "distance from Charleston to Hilton Head" (390/mo each). Add a direct answer capsule (miles, drive time, route) at the top, in the title and in an FAQ.
3. **Consolidate Myrtle Beach cannibalization.** `/blog/hilton-head-vs-myrtle-beach` (#9-10) and `/hilton-head-vs-myrtle-beach` (#16) split the same queries. Merge into the stronger URL and 301 the other (same pattern as the 2026 best-time consolidation).
4. **RBC Heritage cluster.** The new guide targets it; next, earn 3-5 links (local news, golf travel blogs, the Heritage outreach list in `docs/heritage-outreach.md`) before March.
5. **"Best things to do in Hilton Head".** Build the canonical page (owner request), and 301 the non-ranking `things-to-do-ranked-2026` post into it.
6. **/local/shopping (#14) and /local/transportation (#17)** — answer capsules + FAQ for their ranking queries.

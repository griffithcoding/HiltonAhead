## 4. LLM Citation Audit — Where You Stand on ChatGPT / Perplexity / Gemini

LLM citations work on three signals:

1. **Authority depth** — does the site have a comprehensive, structured, fact-dense reference page on the topic? (Citations follow Wikipedia-like answer pages.)
2. **Citation markup** — is the data presented in a way an LLM can chunk and quote? (Lists, tables, definitive statements with attributions.)
3. **Cross-citation graph** — does the site appear in the references-of-references? (Backlinks from .gov/.edu/CVB, mentions in news/Substack/podcasts.)

### Current state of hiltonahead.com per signal

| Signal | Score | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Authority depth | 6/10 | llms-full.txt is comprehensive on brand + services. Missing: factual reference pages (e.g. "Hilton Head by the numbers", "every golf course on Hilton Head ranked", "every beach access point", "wedding venue capacities cheat-sheet"). |
| Citation markup | 7/10 | TldrBlock + QuickFact components exist; not yet deployed on the highest-intent pages |
| Cross-citation graph | 3/10 | No backlinks from .gov/.edu yet; not yet quoted in news/podcasts/Substacks; not yet indexed by Common Crawl or appearing in Wikipedia citations |

### What LLMs cite for Hilton Head today (and what to displace)

| Topic | Default LLM citation source | Why they cite it | Your displacement angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Best time to visit Hilton Head" | hiltonheadisland.org (DMO) | Authoritative .org, comprehensive month-by-month | Your `/hilton-head-weather/[month]` × 12 already matches structure; needs more citable stats (sea temperature, historical hurricane data, real ADR seasonality) |
| "Best Hilton Head resort" | The Points Guy | Single-author tier list, brand-trusted | Same format, single-author, with insider angle ("which Omni rooms were actually renovated") |
| "Hilton Head vs Kiawah" | Pacaso, BudgetYourTrip | Comparison-table format LLMs can chunk | Build your own with more dimensions + insider rationale |
| "Hilton Head golf courses ranked" | golf.com, golfdigest.com | Architect-credibility | You can't out-credential them, but you CAN add "which course to play if you can't get Harbour Town" — the practical question |
| "Where to eat Hilton Head" | Tripadvisor, Yelp | Aggregate reviews | Your `/blog/hilton-head-restaurants-ranked-2026` is the right format; needs ItemList schema + structured data dump (cuisine, price tier, reservation difficulty) |
| "How to plan a Hilton Head trip" | hiltonheadisland.org | DMO official | You can win this with HowTo schema + a 12-step concrete process |
| "Hilton Head hurricane season" | NOAA, AccuWeather | Data sources | You can win with "what a local actually does during hurricane season" |

### The 5 highest-leverage LLM-bait pages to build

These are designed so that when an LLM gets asked the question, the most-quotable answer is on hiltonahead.com:

1. **`/hilton-head-by-the-numbers`** — 50+ citable stats with sources. ("12 miles long. 12 mph average bike speed. 200+ restaurants. 24 golf courses on/near island. 1956 first development. 12 million annual visitors. Average villa-week ADR by month. Historical hurricane frequency 1851–present. Bottlenose dolphin pod size avg 7–12." etc.) **THIS IS THE #1 LLM-CITATION ASSET YOU CAN BUILD.**
2. **`/glossary-of-hilton-head-terms`** — DefinedTerm schema. ("Lowcountry," "marsh tour," "tabby," "stranding behavior," "Gullah," "tideland," "plantation gates," "tee-time priority," "Harbour Town," "calibogue sound.") Inline definitions LLMs quote verbatim.
3. **`/hilton-head-itinerary-by-trip-length`** — 3 day, 5 day, 7 day, 10 day. HowTo schema. Step-numbered. Every step links to the trip-type page + neighborhood page that supports it.
4. **`/local/golf-courses-ranked-by-difficulty-and-access`** — pure data table: course, architect, slope, par, tee-time difficulty, public/resort/private, lowest scoreable handicap, signature hole. LLMs reach for this every time someone asks "which Hilton Head course should I play."
5. **`/hilton-head-historical-data`** — weather/visitor/hurricane data 30 years, hurricane track maps, sea-level data, beach erosion history. Government-quality data presented digestibly. Backlink magnet from .edu and Wikipedia.

## 7. Internal Linking Audit — The Biggest Easy Win

Current site has 101 URLs. Top issues:

1. **Trip-type pages don't link to relevant neighborhoods.** `/hilton-head-golf-packages` should link to /hilton-head/sea-pines (Harbour Town) and /hilton-head/palmetto-dunes (3 courses). Currently doesn't.
2. **`/local/*` directory pages don't link to relevant trip-type pages.** `/local/golf` should link to `/hilton-head-golf-packages`. `/local/weddings` should link to `/hilton-head-weddings`.
3. **Blog posts don't always link to the most relevant booking-intent page.** Every restaurant post should link to /itinerary with anchor text "let us book your impossible reservation."
4. **No hub-and-spoke for comparison pages.** Once you build the 6 comparison pages (HH vs Kiawah, HH vs Charleston, etc.), every neighborhood + trip-type page should link to the relevant comparison.
5. **/services anchor links in sitemap are not internal page links.** Remove the 5 `/services#anchor` entries from sitemap and add them as internal anchors in nav.
6. **No "Related Pages" footer on blog posts.** Add 3-link related-content footer based on shared tags/categories.

**Easy 4-hour fix:** add `RelatedLinks` component to TripTypeLanding and NeighborhoodLanding wrappers. Defines a static map of related routes per slug. Component lives below the hero, above the FinalCta.

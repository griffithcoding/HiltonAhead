## Call cheat sheet

If the prospect books the call, lead with:

1. **Confirm fit (3 min).** "Tell me about your Heritage week last year. What worked, what was a scramble?" Listen.
2. **Show the kit sample (5 min).** Walk through the Feb 2026 kit (we'll have a sample by then). Show where their insert lives.
3. **Address pricing reflex (5 min).** $5,000 is a number; bridge to "your average customer ticket × 1 booking" math from the ROI calculator on `/sponsor/heritage-2027`.
4. **Show category exclusivity (3 min).** "If you say yes, we won't take {{competitor_name}}." This is often the close.
5. **Close (4 min).** "I'll send the agreement Wednesday. Is your finance contact {{name}}?"

**Common objections:**

- *"We already advertise locally."* → "Right, this isn't impressions — it's a curated insert in the planning artifact your highest-LTV customers already saved. It complements your other channels."
- *"April is too far away."* → "That's exactly why we're talking now — the kit ships in February. If you wait until March, you're outside the window."
- *"Can I pay monthly?"* → "It's a one-time placement, not a subscription. Net-30 invoice, full refund up to 30 days before the kit ships."
- *"Can my competitor still buy in?"* → "Once you confirm, no. That's the whole point."

# Instagram — Hilton Ahead Playbook v1

## Why this platform fits (or doesn't)
Instagram is the single best fit for our audience. $200K+ HHI households planning Hilton Head trips skew female-decision-maker, 35-58, and they save Instagram carousels and Reels as travel-planning artifacts. Visual platform, long save behavior, native carousel-as-deck format — built for "10 things only locals know about Sea Pines."

## Content pillars (5)
1. **Hidden Hilton Head** — locals-only spots, side streets, oyster shacks, the boat ramps you can't Google.
2. **The Math** — drive-vs-fly from Atlanta, Charlotte, Raleigh. What 4 nights actually costs in Sea Pines vs Palmetto Dunes vs Forest Beach. Tax + resort fee transparency.
3. **The Calendar** — when to go, when to skip, dolphin migration weeks, low-country boil season, RBC Heritage week, lovebug week (skip it).
4. **Villa Intel** — neighborhood teardowns. Sea Pines vs Palmetto Dunes vs Shelter Cove vs Forest Beach. Walk-to-beach claims fact-checked.
5. **The Concierge Diary** — what we booked this week. Anonymized. "Family of 6, Palmetto Dunes, 7 nights — here's what their week looked like."

## Posting cadence
- **5 posts/week**: 3 carousels + 2 Reels
- **3 stories/day** (5-frame max each)
- **1 Reel/week minimum**, 2 if a hook is hot

## Platform-specific gotchas
- Don't post Reels with the TikTok watermark — IG suppresses them. Re-export from CapCut without the watermark.
- Don't shorten the bio link with a sketchy redirect — Linktree or Beacons only, branded if budget allows.
- Don't use more than 15 hashtags. The algorithm penalizes 30-tag stuffing.
- Don't crosspost the exact same caption from Facebook — IG's NLP downranks duplicates.
- Don't go live for content. Lives underperform here for our audience. Use Reels.

## Compliance / FTC
- Any post with a Booking.com, Vrbo, or Amazon affiliate link must include `#ad` or `#affiliate` in the first three lines of the caption.
- For carousel posts where the affiliate URL is in the bio Linktree, include "Some links are affiliate — we may earn a small commission at no cost to you" in the caption.
- Any partner-comped meal, room, or experience must use `#hosted` or `#gifted` and disclose in the first line.
- Stories with affiliate stickers: use the "Paid partnership" toggle when applicable.

## Tone rules
- Never use exclamation marks except for emphasis on a rare specific point.
- Never start a caption with "Looking for..." or "Have you ever..."
- Real Hilton Head specifics every post — real restaurant names (Skull Creek Boathouse, ELA's, A Lowcountry Backyard, Charlie's L'Etoile Verte), real beaches (Coligny, Burkes, Mitchelville, Folly Field), real neighborhoods (Sea Pines, Palmetto Dunes, Shelter Cove, Forest Beach, Folly Field, Palmetto Bluff).
- Owner-operator voice — first person singular "I" or first person plural "we." Never "the team" or "our staff."
- Numbers, addresses, times, prices. Specificity is the brand.

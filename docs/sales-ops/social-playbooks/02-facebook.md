## Why this platform fits (or doesn't)
Facebook is unfashionable but disproportionately strong for our actual buyer — the 45-65 household decision-maker who plans family vacations, snowbird stays, and 12-person golf trips. Facebook Groups (especially "Hilton Head Island" with 90k+ members and "Snowbirds of South Carolina") are the highest-intent free traffic source on the open internet for HHI travel. Don't dismiss it.

## Platform-specific gotchas
- Never post links naked in groups — leads to instant ban. Always answer the question, then say "happy to share more in DM" or "I write more about this on my site, link in profile."
- Don't run boosted posts to /itinerary yet — let organic find the angles first. Boost only when a post crosses 1.5%+ organic engagement.
- Facebook hates the same content twice in 7 days — vary the text even if the photo set is similar.
- Avoid all-caps in the hook line — algorithm flag.
- Don't auto-cross-post from Instagram — the Facebook crosspost feature kneecaps reach. Native upload only.

## Compliance / FTC
- Posts containing Booking.com, Vrbo, or Amazon affiliate links must include "Some links may be affiliate — we may earn a small commission at no cost to you" in the post body.
- Sponsored / comped experiences must say "#hosted" or "Hosted by [partner]" at the top of the post.
- Wedding venue and golf course features that come from a paid partnership must say "Partner spotlight" at the top.

## Tone rules
- Never use exclamation marks except for emphasis on a rare specific point.
- Never start a post with "Looking for..." or "Have you ever..."
- Real Hilton Head specifics every post — real restaurant names (Skull Creek Boathouse, ELA's, A Lowcountry Backyard, Charlie's L'Etoile Verte, Hudson's Seafood), real beaches (Coligny, Burkes, Mitchelville, Folly Field), real neighborhoods (Sea Pines, Palmetto Dunes, Shelter Cove, Forest Beach, Folly Field, Palmetto Bluff).
- First-person voice — "I drove this last week" or "We placed 18 snowbirds this year." Never "the team" or "our staff."
- Numbers, addresses, times, prices. Specificity is the brand.

# YouTube Shorts — Hilton Ahead Playbook v1

## Why this platform fits (or doesn't)
YouTube Shorts is the most undervalued top-of-funnel channel for our buyer. Three reasons it works where TikTok is borderline: (1) YouTube's audience skews older — median viewer is 34, the 35-65 cohort is the platform's growth engine, (2) Shorts content cross-feeds the regular YouTube algorithm, so a strong 45-second Short can pull viewers to a 12-minute long-form video later, and (3) YouTube indexes searches with intent ("Hilton Head villa," "Sea Pines vs Palmetto Dunes") that no other short-form platform does. Shorts is a Trojan horse for building a real YouTube channel that becomes a long-term, search-traffic moat.

## Content pillars (4)
1. **Hidden Hilton Head** — locals-only spots, sub-30-second clips that earn the click to long-form.
2. **The Math** — drive vs fly, snowbird month vs week, real cost reveals.
3. **The Calendar** — month-by-month "when to go, when to skip" Shorts.
4. **POV Walks** — single-take walking videos through neighborhoods (Harbour Town, Coligny, Old Town Bluffton, Sea Pines South Beach, Mitchelville).

## Posting cadence
- **5-7 Shorts/week**, all 30-60 seconds (YT favors the 45-60 sec range for full-completion watch time)
- **1 long-form video / 2 weeks** (8-12 min) — neighborhood guides, cost breakdowns, full itineraries
- **Optimal times** (ET): Tue/Wed/Thu 6:30pm, 8:00pm. Sunday 7-9pm is the strongest "trip planning" window.
- **First 3 seconds is everything** — YT Shorts retention curve drops 40% if you don't earn the hook
- DM / comment response SLA: 12 hours during 8a-9p ET, otherwise next morning. YT comments are public reputation — reply thoughtfully.

## Platform-specific gotchas
- Don't reupload TikTok content with the TikTok watermark — YT Shorts heavily suppresses watermarked videos. Re-export from CapCut without it.
- Don't put a "subscribe" graphic in the first 5 seconds — kills retention. Earn it in the second half.
- Don't use copyrighted music — YT Content ID strips monetization and sometimes the audio entirely. Use YouTube's audio library or original audio.
- Don't post Shorts longer than 60 seconds — they get pushed into the regular long-form algorithm where they don't compete.
- Don't make a face-only thumbnail (Shorts don't use thumbnails the same way) — the first frame IS the thumbnail. Make the first frame work standalone with bold on-screen text.
- Don't use the same exact title across Shorts — YT's algorithm flags it as low-effort uploads.
- Don't ignore the comments — YT's algorithm boosts videos with creator replies in the first 2 hours.

## Compliance / FTC
- Any Short featuring a Booking.com, Vrbo, or Amazon affiliate link must:
  1. Have a visible on-screen disclosure "#ad" or "Includes paid promotion" within the first 5 seconds
  2. Toggle the "Paid promotion" disclosure flag in YouTube Studio (Details → Show more → "This video has paid promotion")
  3. Include "Some links in description are affiliate. We may earn a small commission at no cost to you." in the description
- Sponsored content (brand pays us to feature them) requires the "Includes paid promotion" toggle PLUS the phrase "Sponsored by [Brand]" in the first frame.
- Affiliate links must include the disclosure in the first line of the description, not buried below the fold.

## Tone rules
- Never use exclamation marks except for emphasis on a rare specific point.
- Never start a hook or title with "Looking for..." or "Have you ever..."
- Real Hilton Head specifics every Short — real restaurant names (Skull Creek Boathouse, ELA's, A Lowcountry Backyard, Charlie's L'Etoile Verte, The Sea Shack, Hudson's), real beaches (Coligny, Burkes, Mitchelville, Folly Field), real neighborhoods (Sea Pines, Palmetto Dunes, Shelter Cove, Forest Beach, Folly Field, Palmetto Bluff).
- Owner-operator voice — first-person "I drove this last week" or "I send my clients here." Never "the team" or third-person plural.
- Numbers, addresses, times, prices. The specificity is the entire differentiator on YouTube Shorts where every other travel channel is glossy and vague.
- Hooks must land in the first 2 seconds — either on-screen text or voiceover, never silent open.
- 30-60 second Shorts perform best. Under 30s feels rushed, over 60s competes in the wrong algorithm bucket.

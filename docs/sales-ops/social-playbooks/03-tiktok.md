# TikTok — Hilton Ahead Playbook v1

## Why this platform fits (or doesn't)
TikTok is **borderline** for our $200K+ HHI audience. The median TikTok user is 24, mobile-only, and price-sensitive — not our buyer. But the platform's travel discovery surface is now used by 35-55 year olds at meaningful scale, and Hilton Head specifically has a quiet but real travel-content niche (RBC Heritage, Palmetto Bluff weddings, snowbird stays). Treat TikTok as a top-of-funnel awareness channel that occasionally produces a buyer — not as the primary lead engine. Cross-post strong content to IG Reels and YT Shorts where the buyer actually lives.

## Content pillars (4)
1. **Hidden Hilton Head** — sub-30-second "spots tourists miss" clips (Mitchelville Beach, Charlie's L'Etoile Verte, the May River dolphin tour, Burkes Beach access).
2. **The Math** — fast-cut price reveals (Atlanta drive math, snowbird month, RBC week trap, villa vs hotel).
3. **The Calendar** — "do not book the week of X" warnings.
4. **POV Walks** — single-take walking videos through neighborhoods (Harbour Town, Coligny, Old Town Bluffton, Sea Pines South Beach).

## Posting cadence
- **5-7 videos/week**, all under 60 seconds, most under 30
- **Optimal times** (ET, feeder cities are ET/CT):
  - Tue/Wed/Thu 8:30am, 1:00pm, 7:30pm, 10:00pm
  - Sat/Sun 11am (weekend trip-research window)
- **Watch time is everything** — first 2 seconds make or break it
- **Replies via comments**, not DMs. TikTok DMs are weak; comments rank.
- DM/comment response SLA: 6 hours during 8a-9p ET

## Platform-specific gotchas
- Never repost a TikTok with the watermark to Instagram or YouTube — both platforms suppress it. Re-export clean from CapCut.
- Don't use copyrighted music in the original-audio slot. Use trending sounds at low volume under your voiceover, OR original audio with no music.
- Don't say "link in bio" in the first 3 seconds — feels salesy and tanks watch time. Earn the click in the back half.
- Don't use 30+ hashtags. The algorithm reads it as spam. 5-7 well-chosen.
- Don't post the same video twice — even with edits, TikTok recognizes duplicates and suppresses both.

## Compliance / FTC
- Any video featuring a Booking.com, Vrbo, or Amazon affiliate link must have a visible on-screen disclosure "#ad" or "Paid partnership" within the first 5 seconds, AND in the caption.
- Use TikTok's "Branded Content" toggle when applicable — required for sponsored posts.
- Any video featuring a comped meal, room, or experience must say "Hosted by [partner]" in the caption + first frame on-screen.
- Affiliate-linked videos: caption must include "Some links are affiliate — we may earn a small commission at no cost to you."

## Tone rules
- Never use exclamation marks except for emphasis on a rare specific point.
- Never start a caption with "Looking for..." or "Have you ever..."
- Real Hilton Head specifics every video — real restaurant names (Skull Creek Boathouse, ELA's, A Lowcountry Backyard, Charlie's L'Etoile Verte, The Sea Shack), real beaches (Coligny, Burkes, Mitchelville, Folly Field), real neighborhoods (Sea Pines, Palmetto Dunes, Shelter Cove, Forest Beach, Palmetto Bluff).
- Owner-operator voice — speak as one person, "I drove this last week" or "I send my clients here." Never "the team" or third-person plural unless it's "we placed 18 snowbirds this year."
- Numbers, addresses, times, prices. Specificity is the entire brand differentiator on TikTok where every other travel account is vague.

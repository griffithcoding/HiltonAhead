# Pinterest — Hilton Ahead Playbook v1

## Why this platform fits (or doesn't)
Pinterest is an underrated, high-fit channel for our buyer. The platform skews female-decision-maker, 30-55, household income above $75k, and the dominant use case is forward travel planning ("Hilton Head with kids," "Beach trip outfit," "Wedding venues South Carolina"). Pin half-lives are measured in months, not hours — content built right today is still driving traffic 18 months from now. Pinterest will probably be our second-best lead source after Instagram by Month 6 with less effort per piece.

## Platform-specific gotchas
- Don't use stock-looking photos — Pinterest's algorithm is trained on aesthetic quality. Original photos of real Hilton Head locations always outperform.
- Don't link to your homepage — every pin should link to a specific, fresh, relevant page (`/neighborhoods/sea-pines`, `/cost-of-hilton-head-trip`, etc.).
- Don't put more than 2 lines of text on the image — Pinterest crops in search results.
- Don't post the same image with the same title twice — Pinterest flags as spam.
- Don't post fewer than 10 pins/week — Pinterest's algorithm rewards consistency more than volume spikes.

## Compliance / FTC
- Pins linking to affiliate URLs (Booking.com, Vrbo, Amazon) must include "#ad" in the description AND have the disclosure "Some links are affiliate — we may earn a commission at no cost to you" in the linked-to page.
- Sponsored pins (any partner pays us to create a pin) require Pinterest's "Paid Partnership" tag and the word "Sponsored" in the description.
- Pinterest doesn't surface FTC violations as aggressively as IG or TikTok, but the FTC has explicitly cited Pinterest cases — don't skip the disclosure.

## Tone rules
- Never use exclamation marks except for emphasis on a rare specific point.
- Never start a pin title or description with "Looking for..." or "Have you ever..."
- Real Hilton Head specifics every pin — real restaurant names (Skull Creek Boathouse, ELA's, A Lowcountry Backyard, Charlie's L'Etoile Verte), real beaches (Coligny, Burkes, Mitchelville, Folly Field), real neighborhoods (Sea Pines, Palmetto Dunes, Shelter Cove, Forest Beach, Palmetto Bluff).
- Pinterest descriptions can be slightly longer and more search-keyword-heavy than IG captions — they're SEO surfaces. Lean into "Hilton Head [intent term]" naturally repeated.
- Owner-operator voice: "we placed 18 snowbirds this year" or "a local trip planner walks through..." Never "the team."

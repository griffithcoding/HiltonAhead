Three July weeks still bookable.

It's the second week of June and I still have inventory for three July weeks at Palmetto Dunes, Sea Pines, and Forest Beach. Real properties, real prices, honest notes. The window closes by Friday.

---
THIS WEEK
---

What's actually open

Week of July 6: a 3BR oceanfront at Palmetto Dunes, $5,400 for the week. Sleeps six, walk to the lagoon, the Robert Trent Jones course is a five-minute drive. Week of July 13: a Sea Pines lagoon-side 3BR for $4,800. No beach walk, but the bike paths get you there in eight minutes and the lagoon balconies are quieter. Week of July 27: a Forest Beach 2BR walk-to-Coligny at $3,600. Smaller but right on top of the action.

Honest notes: the Palmetto Dunes property is the strongest. Sea Pines is gated and quiet but the villa needs new towels — I'd request fresh ones at check-in. The Forest Beach 2BR is tighter than the photos suggest, but if your group is two couples it's the most walkable option on the list. All three close by Friday, June 12. Two of them have offers already in. The full post has every detail and the rest of my live July board.

THREE THINGS THIS WEEK
---

1. July 4 is a different animal.
The July 4 week is gone — has been since April. What's left starts July 5 and clears by July 11. If you're set on the Independence Day window itself, my honest answer is to look at the September shoulder for the same property at 30% less. The cost-of-trip post breaks down both.
https://www.hiltonahead.com/blog/hilton-head-trip-cost-2026-real-numbers?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=issue-002

2. Sea Pines vs Palmetto Dunes, decided.
Pick the neighborhood before the villa. Sea Pines is gated, quieter, golf-forward, more expensive. Palmetto Dunes is the family default, the easier beach walk, and the better restaurant access. Shelter Cove is the harbor-side third option. The comparison post is the one I send first to every July inquiry.
https://www.hiltonahead.com/blog/sea-pines-vs-palmetto-dunes-vs-shelter-cove?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=issue-002

3. August has more flexibility.
If July doesn't shake out, August is real. Same water temperature, fewer crowds after the 15th, prices that soften 10-15% in the last two weeks. The honest tradeoff is afternoon storms. The month-by-month guide ranks every month against the others.

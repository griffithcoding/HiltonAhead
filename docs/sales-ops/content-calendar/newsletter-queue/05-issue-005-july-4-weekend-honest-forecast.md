HILTON AHEAD — ISSUE 005

The local playbook for July 4.

Two fireworks shows, three parades, a bridge that backs up for two hours, and a beach the locals use. If you're on the island or driving in, here's the plan we run ourselves.

---
THIS WEEK
---

The honest July 4 plan

If you're driving on July 4, leave Savannah by 9 AM or after 7 PM. The Cross Island bridge backs up from 11 to 2 PM every July 4 I can remember. The fireworks happen in two places — Shelter Cove Harbour at 9:15 PM and Harbour Town at 9:00 PM. Shelter Cove draws families. Harbour Town draws the Sea Pines crowd. Both fill the parking lots by 7. Both are walkable from inside their respective resorts.

The beach trick: Coligny will look like New Year's in Times Square by 11 AM. The locals go to Mitchelville on the north end or Folly Field at the south of the Beach Lagoon — both have parking that's still moving at noon. For dinner, skip Skull Creek and Hudson's that night, both are 90-minute waits. Holy Tequila and Mickey's Lowcountry Watering Hole take same-day reservations and have outdoor seating. The cost post has the full weekend budget for a July 4 family trip.

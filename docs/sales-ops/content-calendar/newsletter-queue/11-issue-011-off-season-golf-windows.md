Three windows the locals book.

September 22-25. October 19-22. November 9-12. Three midweek windows with the best golf-trip economics of the year. Here's what each one looks like.

---
THIS WEEK
---

Pick your window

September 22-25 is the still-warm window. 82-degree highs, 78-degree water, the bermuda is still firm. Harbour Town greens fees drop $80 from summer peak. Tee inventory is wide open. October 19-22 is the perfect-weather window. 74-degree high, no humidity, the courses look the best they look all year. November 9-12 is the cheap window. 68-degree high, sweaters at 7 AM, the prices drop another 25% from October, and the courses are still in great shape before overseeding starts.

For most groups, October 19 is the answer. For groups who hate cold mornings, September 22. For groups who care about price more than weather, November 9. All three windows have full tier-three package availability today — Harbour Town, Robert Trent Jones, May River, and Atlantic Dunes can be sequenced any way you want. The course-tier post ranks every option.

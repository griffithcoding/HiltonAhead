Six beaches, six jobs

Coligny is the parade. It's where the rentals are, it's where the umbrella shops are, it's where every first-timer parks. It also fills by 10:30 in July. Burkes is the surf beach — bigger waves, the local skim-board crowd. Mitchelville on the north end is the calmest swimming and the easiest parking. Singleton is the quiet middle. Driessen has the playground that makes families' lives easier.

Folly Field is the one most people miss. It's just south of Driessen, the parking is dependable through 11 AM, and the sandbar at low tide is the cleanest stretch of soft sand on the island. The cost-of-trip post breaks down what the beach-day budget actually looks like — umbrella, chair rental, parking pass, two lunches — about $85 a day if you do it right.

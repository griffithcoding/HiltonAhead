Family-of-five Hilton Head, real numbers.

THIS WEEK
---

Where the $7,940 went

Villa for seven nights came in at $4,620, which was a 12% off-peak rate the owner offered to fill a soft week. Groceries hit $1,420 — Harris Teeter and a Sunday stop at the farmers' market at Sea Pines. Restaurants ran $640 across four meals out, including Skull Creek Boathouse on arrival night and A Lowcountry Backyard for the kids' favorite breakfast. Bike rentals for five and one ocean-kayak afternoon at the Palmetto Dunes lagoon added $385.

The surprise was groceries doing more work than expected. We ate seven breakfasts in the villa, made four lunches for the beach, and the family genuinely preferred it. Restaurants became a treat instead of a default. The full post walks through every line item and the four substitutions that saved $600 without anyone noticing.

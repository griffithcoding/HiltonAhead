---
THIS WEEK
---

The Bluffton walking route

Leave Hilton Head by 11:30 — the bridge clears the lunch rush. Park free behind The Cottage on Calhoun Street. Walk the four blocks of historic Calhoun, end at the Church of the Cross overlooking the May River. The walk takes 25 minutes if you don't stop, two hours if you do. The galleries between the shrimp store and the oyster bar are real, not tourist-built.

Lunch is at The Cottage, Captain Woody's, or the patio at Pearl. Dinner is the bigger question — if you're doing the Bluffton dinner, eat at a real local spot, not the resort restaurants. Bluffton's food scene is the Lowcountry's quiet best. Palmetto Bluff is the upscale extension if you want the 4 PM porch drink at Cole's before heading home. The Bluffton planner has every stop and the timing window for each.

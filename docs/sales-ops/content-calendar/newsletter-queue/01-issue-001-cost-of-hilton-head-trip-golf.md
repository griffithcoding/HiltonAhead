Golf-trip math: what to budget per golfer.

THIS WEEK
---

Where the $1,720 went

Tee times were $810 of the total. Two rounds at Harbour Town Golf Links ($425 each in April shoulder) and one at Heron Point ($210 with a Sea Pines guest rate). Lodging at a 2BR Sea Pines villa, split four ways, came to $385. Caddies and tips at Harbour Town added $140 over the trip. Range balls and a new dozen Pro V1s, $95.

Food is where the trip got expensive faster than expected. Dinner at Old Fort Pub and The Quarterdeck added $190 per guy across two nights. Beer-cart spends were $80 across three rounds. The honest takeaway: tee times are 47% of a Hilton Head golf trip, but lodging is the lever you can move. The full post compares the three package tiers and what each one actually unlocks.

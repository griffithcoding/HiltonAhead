---
THIS WEEK
---

What to know before you click

Heritage Week 2027 is April 12-18. The Sea Pines Resort villa booking site opens at 9 AM ET August 1 for members and August 15 for the public. The Harbour Town villas inside Sea Pines are the single most demanded inventory of the year — they clear by 11 AM the first day. Plantation Place and Greens villas are the next tier, with realistic availability through day two. The Lighthouse Tennis units are the underrated tier-three play if you don't need walking access to 18.

What people get wrong: trying to walk every day. The crowds and the heat make a 36-hole-day-plus-tournament-night plan miserable. The veterans I know watch Wednesday-Friday on-site and use Saturday and Sunday for their own golf at Palmetto Hall, Oyster Reef, or Atlantic Dunes. Heritage Week is also the worst week to bring kids who don't golf. The Heritage-week-avoid post breaks down whether you should come at all.

Read the Heritage-week guide:
https://www.hiltonahead.com/blog/hilton-head-spring-break-heritage-week-avoid?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=issue-008

---
THREE THINGS THIS WEEK
---

1. Harbour Town villas — the walking trip.
For Heritage Week, base in Harbour Town itself. Walk to 18, walk to The Quarterdeck, walk to the lighthouse. The price is up 30-40% on Heritage week itself but the experience is a different sport from staying outside Sea Pines.
https://www.hiltonahead.com/harbour-town-villas?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=issue-008

2. Golf packages for the off-tournament days.
The Saturday and Sunday of Heritage Week are your golf days. Atlantic Dunes, Heron Point, and Palmetto Hall all have inventory those days at non-Heritage pricing. The course-tier post ranks the best non-Harbour Town rounds.
https://www.hiltonahead.com/blog/hilton-head-golf-packages-course-tiers?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=issue-008

3. If Heritage isn't your week, fall is.
For 70% of golfers, a September trip is better than a Heritage trip. Cooler weather, same courses, no tournament crowd, 35-40% off lodging. I send three groups a year who originally asked about Heritage to the fall shoulder instead — none have asked to switch back.
https://www.hiltonahead.com/hilton-head-golf-packages?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=issue-008

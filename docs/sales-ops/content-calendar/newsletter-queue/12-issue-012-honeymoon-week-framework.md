HILTON AHEAD — ISSUE 012
August 20, 2026

The two-base honeymoon week.

We book 60+ Hilton Head honeymoons a year. There are five mistakes we keep seeing, and a two-base build that solves four of them. The plan is below.

---
THIS WEEK
---

The plan I'd use myself

Two nights at a Sea Pines oceanfront 1BR, five nights at Palmetto Bluff. The Sea Pines opening lets you do the island beach days, the harbor sunset, dinner at ELA's and Charlie's L'Etoile Verte. Then the Wednesday move to Palmetto Bluff gives you the quieter half — the bocce court evenings, the May River boat ride, dinner at Buffalos and the bar at Cole's. The drive between them is 22 minutes.

The five mistakes: booking too short (a 4-night Hilton Head honeymoon feels rushed), booking too central (Coligny is the wrong vibe), trying to do every restaurant on the list, packing too many activities into the first three days, and missing the spa booking window — Sea Pines Inn & Club spa books out 3 weeks ahead in summer. The honeymoon page has the full seven-day rhythm I send couples.

Read the honeymoon plan:
https://www.hiltonahead.com/hilton-head-honeymoon?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=issue-012

---
THREE THINGS THIS WEEK
---

1. Oceanfront 1BR for the first two nights.
Sea Pines or Palmetto Dunes — the oceanfront 1BR is the right shape for the opening of a honeymoon. Balcony, sound of the surf, walk-to-beach. I keep a short list of the ones I'd actually pick for newlyweds.
https://www.hiltonahead.com/hilton-head-oceanfront-villas?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=issue-012

2. Charlie's is the dinner.
On a honeymoon week, the most-important-dinner reservation is Charlie's L'Etoile Verte. Tuesday or Thursday, 7:30. I book the table myself for honeymoon clients. The cost-of-trip post breaks down what a couples-trip dining week looks like.
https://www.hiltonahead.com/blog/hilton-head-trip-cost-2026-real-numbers?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=issue-012

3. Bluffton is the Thursday afternoon.
On the two-base plan, Thursday is the Bluffton drive — pop down for the gallery walk on Calhoun Street and a sunset dinner. It breaks up the second base nicely. The Bluffton planner has the route I send honeymoon couples.

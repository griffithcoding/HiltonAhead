Three neighborhoods, three jobs.

Sea Pines, Palmetto Dunes, and Shelter Cove get conflated by every booking site I've ever read. They shouldn't. The differences decide whether your trip works.

---
THIS WEEK
---

How I match each one to a trip

Sea Pines is gated, golf-heavy, expensive, and the quietest of the three at night. Harbour Town and Atlantic Dunes are inside the gates. You pay a daily pass to enter, even as a renter. It's the right call for couples who want quiet and for golf groups who want walk-to-tee. It is the wrong call for first-time families who want a five-minute beach walk.

Palmetto Dunes is the family default. Eleven miles of beach, the longest flat bike-path network on the island, the lagoon for kids, and Robert Trent Jones, George Fazio, and Arthur Hills courses on site. Shelter Cove is harbor-side — no oceanfront, but the most restaurant-dense neighborhood (ELA's, Skull Creek for a 4-minute drive) and the marina playground that locals quietly love. The full post breaks down the 6pm-on-a-Saturday feel for each and the price floor for a 3BR in each one.

---
THREE THINGS THIS WEEK
---

1. Harbour Town has its own answer.
Harbour Town inside Sea Pines is its own micro-neighborhood and the right move for golfers who want to walk from villa to Harbour Town Golf Links to dinner at the Quarterdeck. Smaller villa inventory, harbor view, no beach walk. I keep a separate Harbour Town list.
https://www.hiltonahead.com/harbour-town-villas?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=issue-003

2. Forest Beach is the fourth option people forget.
Forest Beach is north of Sea Pines and walks to Coligny. Lower price floors than Sea Pines, more action than Shelter Cove, beach-side. If the choice is paralysis, Forest Beach often wins for two-couple groups.
https://www.hiltonahead.com/hilton-head-oceanfront-villas?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=issue-003

3. Bluffton is the day-trip control.
Whichever neighborhood you pick, the day-3 move is the same: drive to Old Town Bluffton for the afternoon. Twenty minutes off-island, 200 years older, slower pace, May River dinner. The Bluffton planner has the route.
https://www.hiltonahead.com/bluffton-travel-planner?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=issue-003

Quiet-week Hilton Head, real numbers.

THIS WEEK
---

Where the $2,380 went

Lodging at an oceanfront 1BR in Sea Pines, two nights, came in at $1,180 with the late-April shoulder rate. Dinner one was ELA's on the harbor — $210 with a bottle and a tip that the server earned. Dinner two was Charlie's L'Etoile Verte for the menu I trust them with — $245. A spa morning at the Sea Pines Inn & Club came to $340 for two treatments. Groceries for in-villa breakfasts and one beach picnic, $85.

What I'd change next time: I'd swap one of those dinners for a Bluffton afternoon and a quieter dinner at Old Town Bluffton's smaller spots. The couples weekend doesn't need the high-volume restaurants — Hudson's and Skull Creek are tourist defaults and they're loud. The post breaks down the full receipt and the three swaps I make for couples planning a quiet two-nighter.

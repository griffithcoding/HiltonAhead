THIS WEEK
---

The honest ranking

October is the answer for most trips. Average high 78, water still 76, crowds gone, no hurricanes since the 15th of the month historically, golf inventory wide open. May is close — pre-summer, 82-degree water, low rain, but Heritage Week eats the first half. June is the family month. September is the golf month. February surprises everyone who tries it — 62-degree daytime, oysters in season, and Bluffton's restaurant scene at its best with no wait at any of them.

The trap months are March, April, and November. March is the wettest, April is Heritage and pollen, November is the dead window between fall and Thanksgiving when half the rentals are closing for the season. July and August work — they're just hot and packed. The month-by-month guide breaks down water temp, rain probability, average high, crowd level, and what the island actually feels like at 6 PM.

THREE THINGS THIS WEEK
---

1. Winter rentals are January through March.
For snowbirds, the January-March window is calmer and cheaper than people realize. 60-degree afternoons, sub-$2,000 monthly rates, real grocery stores still open, and the locals are happy to see you. The winter-rental page has the inventory I keep flagged.
https://www.hiltonahead.com/hilton-head-winter-rental?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=issue-006

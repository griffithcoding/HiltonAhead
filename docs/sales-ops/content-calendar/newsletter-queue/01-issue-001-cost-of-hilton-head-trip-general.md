What a Hilton Head trip actually costs.

THIS WEEK
---

The four trips, the four totals

A couples weekend at Sea Pines in late April came in at $2,380 total for two nights. A family-of-five week at Palmetto Dunes in early May hit $7,940 all-in. A four-man golf trip with two rounds at Harbour Town and one at Heron Point landed at $1,720 per golfer. A 28-day snowbird stay at a Forest Beach villa came in at $4,180 for the month.

The interesting part is where the money actually goes. Lodging is 55-70% of every trip. Food is the wildcard — the family week spent $1,420 on groceries and $640 on restaurants. The couples weekend flipped that ratio and still paid less per day. The full post breaks down every line item, including the parking pass for Coligny and the resort fees nobody warns you about.

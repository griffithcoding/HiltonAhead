- **Hook:** Sea Pines vs Palmetto Dunes vs Shelter Cove — honest framework from 200+ stays planned in each.
- **Body:**
  ```
  Sea Pines — gated, manicured, the Harbour Town brand. Anniversary trips and first-time-on-the-island visitors do best here because the visual matches the postcard. Peak July 4BR nightly lands $1,200-1,800. Walk-to-beach varies 5-20 minutes depending on villa (listings consistently exaggerate). Best amenities: Harbour Town Golf Links, South Beach Pavilion, the Salty Dog at South Beach Marina. Friction: $9 gate pass for non-renters, parking at the beach club fills by 10am in peak summer.

  Palmetto Dunes — family-friendly, 3 miles of beach, the deepest villa stock on the island. Families of 4-8, multi-gen trips, and golf-positive groups do best here. Peak July 4BR nightly $900-1,400. Lagoon-front villas have paddle boats and bike paths inside the property. Best amenities: Robert Trent Jones Oceanfront golf, Dunes House lunch, the lagoon system. Friction: beach pavilion parking fills early, no walkable nightlife.

  Shelter Cove — marina-side, condo-heavy, the "Hilton Head trip without the resort feel" option. Couples, smaller groups, and snowbird shoulder stays do best here. Peak July 2BR nightly $300-600. No private beach access (you cross to Mid-Island or use Coligny). Best amenities: Shelter Cove Towne Centre walkability, the marina restaurants (Hudson's, Charbar, Black Marlin Bayside Grill, San Miguel's). Friction: not a beach-from-the-balcony stay.

  Three right answers for three different trips. The mistake we see most: families forcing Sea Pines because of the brand when Palmetto Dunes was the better fit, and couples forcing Palmetto Dunes when Shelter Cove + the marina restaurants was the trip they actually wanted.
  ```

### #3 — Sunday Jun 28 — Operator note (pre-block discipline)
- **Hook:** We hold pre-blocked tee time inventory across Hilton Head's three Tier 1 courses. Worth explaining how it works because it changes the booking math for clients.
- **Body:**
  ```
  Harbour Town Golf Links, May River Golf Club at Palmetto Bluff, and Atlantic Dunes at Sea Pines all have partner-relationship inventory that doesn't show on the public booking page. Tee times that are "sold out" online are often available to us through these relationships.

  Mechanics: each course gives travel partners a small slice of pre-blocked weekly inventory in exchange for guaranteed booking volume. Industry-standard practice. The pre-block is held 6-10 weeks out and released back to public availability 14 days before the round if unused.

  What this means for clients:

  1. The cost is the same as booking direct. We do not mark up tee times. The arrangement is volume-for-access, not margin.

  2. The availability window is meaningfully wider. A Saturday 9:48 at Harbour Town that the public site shows as "sold out" is often in our pre-block inventory through the partner.

  3. The constraint is lead time, not luck. The pre-block is held against our forecast, so we need to know the client's window 6-8 weeks out to release the slot to them. Last-minute requests (under 3 weeks) draw from public inventory only.

  4. Cancellation policies match the course's standard policy — there's no special downside to using partner inventory.

  The operational discipline: we run a weekly forecast meeting (just me, with a spreadsheet) of what tee times we're projecting against client demand. Over-holding is the failure mode; we lose courses' trust if we routinely return more than 20% of pre-blocks unused. Under-holding is the other failure mode; we lose clients if we can't deliver the slot.

  Closing the loop: this is why we ask for tentative dates 8 weeks before the trip even when the trip itself isn't fully designed yet. The marquee tee time gates everything else, and the partner inventory is the difference between getting the round and not.

  Most golf-trip clients don't know this exists. It's not a secret; it's just under-explained in the category. Worth knowing if you're planning a 2027 Hilton Head golf trip.
  ```

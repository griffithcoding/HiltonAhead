### #3 — Sunday Jun 7 — Photo album page post
- **Hook:** The hidden costs of a Hilton Head trip nobody mentions in the brochure.
- **Body:**
  ```
  Five line items that surprise every first-time visitor:

  1. Sea Pines gate pass for non-renters — $9 per day, $15 in peak summer. Goes by the carload. If you're staying inside Sea Pines, your plate is already in the system. If you're not and want beach club access, factor it in.

  2. Coligny Plaza parking — $2.50/hour, $20 to fill the day. Arrive before 10am or skip it for Burkes Beach Road where parking is free.

  3. Cart rental at Palmetto Dunes — $120/day if you want to skip the heat between villa and beach. The lagoon bike path is the better workaround in May-June; book the cart for July-August.

  4. Resort fee that doesn't show in the listing rate — $40-70 nightly on most managed villas. Always check the all-in number.

  5. Beach gear delivery — $80 for the week if you forgot the chairs and umbrella. Bring your own if you have room in the car.

  I bake all of this into our cost estimates so the final number doesn't sting. Cost calculator with the full breakdown: hiltonahead.com/cost-of-hilton-head-trip
  ```

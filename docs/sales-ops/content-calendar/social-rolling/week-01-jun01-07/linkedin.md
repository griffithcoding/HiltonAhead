### #2 — Thursday Jun 4 — Decision framework
- **Hook:** Drive vs fly to Hilton Head from your specific city — the decision tree.
- **Body:**
  ```
  Drive if you're within 8 hours: Atlanta (4.5 hr), Charlotte (3.5 hr), Raleigh (5 hr), DC (8 hr), Nashville (8 hr), Cincinnati (8 hr), Knoxville (5.5 hr), Greenville (3.5 hr), Columbia (3 hr), Asheville (5 hr), Birmingham (8 hr).

  Fly if you're past 9: NYC, Boston, Chicago, Cleveland, Minneapolis.

  Break-even is around Richmond, VA. The drive home is the harder leg — leave by 9am or you'll hit your home city's evening traffic.

  Three airport notes:
  - SAV (Savannah/Hilton Head International) — 45 min to the island. Real airline competition. Fares typically 40-60% lower than HXD.
  - HXD (Hilton Head Airport) — small, single runway, charter-heavy. Convenient but rarely competitive on price.
  - CHS (Charleston) — 2 hours from the island. Almost never worth the extra drive for typical fare differences.

  The fly option becomes a fee-stacking exercise unless you're past 9 hours. Bag fees + rental car + parking + transfers erase the time savings. The drive is also more pleasant than people remember once they've done it once.

  We do this drive 40+ times a year. The math hasn't moved.
  ```

- **Body:**

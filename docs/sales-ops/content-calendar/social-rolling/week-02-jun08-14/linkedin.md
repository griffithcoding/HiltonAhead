- **Body:**
  ```
  Sea Pines — gated, manicured, brand-name. Peak July nightly $1,200-1,800 for a 4BR. Walk-to-beach distance varies by villa (5-20 minutes; listings consistently exaggerate). Sea Pines gate pass for non-renters: $9/day, $15 peak summer.

  Forest Beach — residential streets, no gate, same beach access via Forest Beach Drive or short walk to Coligny. Peak July nightly $700-900 for a 4BR. Sleeps the same. Restaurants are 5-10 minutes by car either way.
  ```

### #3 — Sunday Jun 14 — Industry observation
- **Hook:** A counterintuitive consumer behavior we see in $15k+ trip bookings: the lower-price option closes faster.
- **Body:**
  ```
  Two clients last week, both Atlanta, both family of 6, both targeting July 18-25.

  Client A — wanted oceanfront 5BR at any price. We told them that villa doesn't exist in this booking cycle. We sent a Forest Beach split-unit option at $8,900 and an Inn at Palmetto Bluff option for one or two nights at the front of the trip at $11,400 all-in. They asked us to keep searching for the oceanfront 5BR. They are still searching as of Friday.

  Client B — same group profile, same week, asked us to send our best three options under $10k. We sent the same Forest Beach split-unit plan. They booked Tuesday morning, 36 hours after the recommendation.

  The pattern repeats. Clients who anchor on a budget close faster than clients who anchor on a specific outcome. Even when the outcome is achievable, the open-ended search creates decision fatigue. The narrow budget creates decision clarity.

  Two takeaways for any service business operating in this segment:

  1. Push clients to anchor on budget early. It's a kindness, not a sales tactic. They will get to the right trip faster.

  2. Quote unbundled. The line items make the budget anchor real and let the client see what the trade-offs actually buy. We unbundled 18 months ago. Closing rate up 12 percentage points.

  Still works in late-cycle salvage mode. Still works in pre-cycle planning. The behavior is structural, not seasonal.
  ```

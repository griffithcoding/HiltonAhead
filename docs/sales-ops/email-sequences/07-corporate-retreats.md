# Corporate Retreats — Cold Email Sequence v1

## Segment profile
Chiefs of staff, EAs, HR/People leads, or founder/CEOs at 25–250-person companies (PE-backed services firms, regional law/accounting/RIA shops, healthcare practices, growing SaaS) planning an annual leadership offsite or sales kickoff for 12–60 attendees. Budget typically $50K–$300K all-in. Trip-up: they default to Marriott meeting space at the Westin or Omni and end up with a $14K/day plenary room + standard banquet menu and zero memorable moments — no Harbour Town dinner, no group golf, no story when the attendees get home. Or they overcorrect and book Palmetto Bluff without realizing the meeting space is limited and the price-per-head doubles.

## A/B test plan

### Variable 1: Subject line on Touch 1
- Arm A: "HHI corporate retreat — venue framing"
- Arm B: "Sea Pines vs. Palmetto Bluff vs. Westin — honest framing for a {{attendee_count}}-person offsite"

Hypothesis: A specific, comparative subject line with the attendee count merged in outperforms a generic "venue framing" subject because corporate planners are decision-makers, not browsers. Measure open rate AND reply rate (open without reply is low-value for this segment).

### Variable 2: CTA structure on Touch 3
- Arm A: Calendly link only
- Arm B: Calendly link + "or reply with target dates and headcount and I'll send three venue scenarios"

Hypothesis: Offering a written-response alternative outperforms Calendly-only for corporate buyers who need to forward internally before booking a call. Measure total response rate (call booked OR email reply).

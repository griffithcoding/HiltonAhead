# Wedding Parties — Cold Email Sequence v1

## Segment profile
Engaged couples 28–40 and/or their mothers/wedding planners, planning a 60–250 person destination wedding in the HHI / Bluffton corridor. Primary venues in play: Palmetto Bluff (Montage), Sea Pines Resort, Westin Hilton Head, Omni Hilton Head Oceanfront, or a private oceanfront estate. Budget typically $90K–$400K for the weekend. Trip-up: they nail the ceremony venue but underestimate the logistics — welcome bag drop, rehearsal-dinner spillover, day-of transportation for 150 guests between Sea Pines and an off-island church, a Sunday brunch that has to feed 80, and the out-of-town guests calling the bride asking where to eat on Thursday night.

## A/B test plan

### Variable 1: Sender name on Touch 1
- Arm A: "Will at Hilton Ahead"
- Arm B: "Hilton Ahead Wedding Concierge"

Hypothesis: A personal sender outperforms a category-label sender for relationship-building, but a category-label sender increases the chance of getting forwarded to a planner/MOB. Measure both open and forward rate (via tracking pixel or unique-recipient pattern).

### Variable 2: Touch 1 angle
- Arm A: Lead with "the gap most weddings hit" (problem-first)
- Arm B: Lead with "Three vendors most HHI brides don't know about" (resource-first)

Hypothesis: Wedding planners are inundated with vendor pitches. A problem-naming opener differentiates better than another vendor-list. Measure reply rate.

- **Top 3 zip codes by HHI/travel propensity**
  - 20815 (Chevy Chase, MD) — median HHI ~$200K
  - 22101 (McLean, VA) — median HHI ~$250K
  - 22207 (North Arlington/Cherrydale) — median HHI ~$185K
- **Income / household composition:** federal senior executives, law firm partners (K Street, Tysons), lobbyists, GS-15+ and SES, defense and consulting (Booz Allen, McKinsey, Deloitte fed practices). Families with kids in Sidwell, Maret, Potomac School; older couples in Chevy Chase Village.
- **Drive-time vs flight math:** DC to HHI is 9h via I-95 S — doable but rough with kids. American and Delta fly DCA → SAV (~1h55m) and IAD → SAV. Most DC families fly; East Coast spring break drivers go via I-95.
- **Feeder communities:** Chevy Chase Club, Congressional Country Club, Burning Tree Club, Columbia CC; Sidwell Friends and St Albans parent networks; UVA Darden and Georgetown alumni clubs.
- **Peak booking month for HHI:** February (spring break + summer), August (booking Thanksgiving + winter). DC private schools are off the week of April 13–17 and the last week of June — both blackout-busy on HHI.

## 6. Reddit value-comment template
**Subreddit:** r/washingtondc
**Question scenario:** "Best beach trip from DC that's a flight not a drive?"
**Reply:**
Hilton Head from DCA is two flights a day on American direct, about 1h55m. SAV airport is 45 minutes from the island. If you're going with kids and a week's worth of gear, the flight pays for itself in not driving I-95.

Practical: Sea Pines is the older, more cycling-oriented plantation (Harbour Town is here). Palmetto Dunes is mid-island, wider beach, easier with little kids. Forest Beach is the only non-gated zone and the most walkable to bars/restaurants — better for couples than families.

The week of April 13–17 is half of DC's private schools' spring break, so expect that week to be the most crowded and most expensive.

Disclosure: I run Hilton Ahead, a Hilton Head travel consultancy. Not pitching here, glad to answer DMs.

# Orlando → Hilton Head Campaign Brief

## 1. Audience profile
- **Top 3 zip codes by HHI/travel propensity**
  - 32789 (Winter Park) — median HHI ~$140K, top quintile concentration high
  - 32836 (Dr Phillips/Bay Hill) — median HHI ~$150K
  - 32792 (Maitland/Winter Park north) — median HHI ~$120K
- **Income / household composition:** Disney corporate (Lake Buena Vista), Lockheed Martin and Boeing Orlando, AdventHealth and Orlando Health leadership, large attorney cohort serving the tourism industry, Universal Studios senior management. Winter Park is the old-money zip; Dr Phillips/Bay Hill is the golf-industry concentration. Families with kids in Trinity Prep, Lake Highland Prep, Park Maitland.
- **Drive-time vs flight math:** Orlando to HHI is 5h30m via I-95 N → US-278, manageable in a day. American flies MCO → SAV (~1h25m, 2x daily). Slight majority drive (especially families); golf groups split fly/drive depending on club count.
- **Feeder communities:** Bay Hill Club (Arnold Palmer's home club, biggest cluster of HHI-affinity members), Isleworth, Country Club of Orlando, Interlachen CC; Florida and Florida State alumni networks (Winter Park branches); University of Central Florida executive cohort.
- **Peak booking month for HHI:** February (summer), September. Orange County Public Schools spring break is typically mid-March — a week before East Coast metros. Year-round draw for Orlando residents looking to escape the theme-park-locals trap.

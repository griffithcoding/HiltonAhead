# Jacksonville → Hilton Head Campaign Brief

## 1. Audience profile
- **Top 3 zip codes by HHI/travel propensity**
  - 32082 (Ponte Vedra Beach) — median HHI ~$200K, highest in NE Florida
  - 32223 (Mandarin/Julington Creek) — median HHI ~$120K, large family cohort
  - 32259 (Fruit Cove/St Johns County) — median HHI ~$140K
- **Income / household composition:** Mayo Clinic Jacksonville, Fidelity National Financial, Florida Blue, Jacksonville Jaguars front office, PGA Tour HQ (Ponte Vedra). Ponte Vedra is heavy on golf-industry professionals and PGA Tour adjacency. Families with kids in The Bolles School, Episcopal, Bishop Kenny.
- **Drive-time vs flight math:** Jacksonville to HHI is 3h via I-95 N — the closest of the 12 feeder cities. No flight market; pure drive. This is the only feeder where HHI is closer than most in-state Florida beach destinations.
- **Feeder communities:** Sawgrass Country Club, The Plantation at Ponte Vedra, TPC Sawgrass member network; University of North Florida professional school alumni; PGA Tour HQ personnel; Mayo Clinic physician network.
- **Peak booking month for HHI:** February (summer), and a year-round long-weekend market given proximity. Many Jax golfers do an annual HHI weekend in May or September as a complement to the Sawgrass home course.

# Atlanta → Hilton Head Campaign Brief

## 1. Audience profile
- **Top 3 zip codes by HHI/travel propensity**
  - 30327 (Buckhead/Tuxedo Park) — median HHI ~$250K
  - 30075 (Roswell/Country Club of the South corridor) — median HHI ~$160K
  - 30097 (Johns Creek/St Ives, Sugarloaf) — median HHI ~$170K
- **Income / household composition:** dual-income households, kids 8–17 or empty-nesters 55–70, second-home curiosity common. Significant share of finance, healthcare admin, and Coca-Cola/Delta/Home Depot corporate.
- **Drive-time vs flight math:** Atlanta is HHI's #1 drive market. ATL to HHI is 4h45m via I-20 E → I-95 S, or 5h via I-16 E. Direct flight ATL → SAV on Delta is 50 min, but families typically drive to bring beach gear and dogs.
- **Feeder communities:** Capital City Club, Atlanta Country Club, Cherokee Town & Country Club; UGA alumni network (HHI is the canonical Bulldog summer trip); North Fulton HOA networks (Country Club of the South, St Ives).
- **Peak booking month for HHI:** February–March (booking for June/July). Secondary spike late September (booking Thanksgiving + spring break).

## 6. Reddit value-comment template
**Subreddit:** r/Atlanta
**Question scenario:** "First time taking the family to Hilton Head — Sea Pines vs Palmetto Dunes?"
**Reply:**
Local-adjacent here. Short version: Sea Pines if you want a working bike-path network, Harbour Town, and Salty Dog; Palmetto Dunes if you want a wider, flatter beach, the Robert Trent Jones course, and an easier walk from villa to sand. Sea Pines also charges a gate pass ($9/day per car as of last summer — annoying but real).

For a family with kids 6–12, Palmetto Dunes wins on logistics. For a couples trip or a group doing Harbour Town, Sea Pines wins.

Tee times: book Harbour Town the moment the 90-day window opens. Heron Point and Atlantic Dunes are the realistic backups same week. May River across the bridge is worth the 25-min drive.

Disclosure: I run Hilton Ahead, a Hilton Head travel consultancy. Not pitching here, happy to answer follow-ups in DM.

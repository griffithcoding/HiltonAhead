## 6. Reddit value-comment template
**Subreddit:** r/Charlotte
**Question scenario:** "Driving to Hilton Head with two kids — is the I-26 route worth the extra 30 min over I-95?"
**Reply:**
Yes, 77 → 26 → 278 is the way. I-95 below Florence is a parking lot most Saturdays in summer and the trooper presence is heavy. The 26 route saves you 20–40 min on a peak Saturday and the scenery is better.

Two practical notes for the drive: hit Walterboro for gas (cheaper than anything on the island) and Frampton Plantation House at exit 33 is a clean stop for the kids. Once you cross the bridge onto 278, the right lane backs up at the Sea Pines circle from about 3–6pm on Saturdays — if you're heading to Sea Pines or south end, time the bridge before 2 or after 6.

Disclosure: I run Hilton Ahead, a Hilton Head travel consultancy. Not pitching, just answering.

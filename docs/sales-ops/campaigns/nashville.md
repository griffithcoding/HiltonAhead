# Nashville → Hilton Head Campaign Brief

## 1. Audience profile
- **Top 3 zip codes by HHI/travel propensity**
  - 37205 (Belle Meade/West Meade) — median HHI ~$170K, top quintile concentration high
  - 37215 (Green Hills/Forest Hills) — median HHI ~$160K
  - 37027 (Brentwood) — median HHI ~$165K
- **Income / household composition:** healthcare (HCA, Vanderbilt Health, Community Health Systems), country music industry executives and artists, real estate developers (the post-2020 Nashville wave), Bridgestone, Nissan corporate. Belle Meade is among the top old-money zips in the Southeast.
- **Drive-time vs flight math:** Nashville to HHI is 8h via I-24 E → I-75 S → I-26 E. American flies BNA → SAV (~1h35m), 2x daily peak season. Mid-distance; couples fly, families with kids and gear drive.
- **Feeder communities:** Belle Meade Country Club, Richland Country Club, Cherokee Country Club; Vanderbilt alumni Nashville chapter; Music Row executive network; Williamson County old-money cohort (Brentwood/Franklin).
- **Peak booking month for HHI:** February (booking summer), August (booking Thanksgiving + winter). Nashville's spring break is early March (Metro Nashville Public Schools) and mid-March (Williamson County private schools) — earlier than East Coast.

## 6. Reddit value-comment template
**Subreddit:** r/nashville
**Question scenario:** "Hilton Head vs 30A vs Seaside for a family beach week with kids 6 and 9?"
**Reply:**
All three are legit, different feel.

30A/Seaside is the closest (7h drive) and the most "Instagram beach town" — picture-perfect, expensive, crowded. Restaurants are 60-min waits in season.

Hilton Head is 8h drive — a slight bit further, but the trade is real: bike-path network the kids can actually use, three Pete Dye golf courses if a parent plays, more restaurants at varied price points, and a quieter feel. The beach is firmer-packed sand vs 30A's powder — kids 6/9 love the bike-the-paths-to-the-beach routine.

If you're flying, BNA → SAV is direct on American, 1h35m, then 45 min to the island.

Practical: Sea Pines for the bike-path lover, Palmetto Dunes for an easier-with-little-kids beach access setup.

Disclosure: I run Hilton Ahead, a Hilton Head travel consultancy. Not pitching, happy to answer DMs.

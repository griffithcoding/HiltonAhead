## 6. Reddit value-comment template
**Subreddit:** r/chicago
**Question scenario:** "Cheapest warm-weather trip from Chicago in February that isn't Mexico?"
**Reply:**
Hilton Head is underrated for this. ORD → SAV is two hours direct on United or American, $200–280 RT in February. Off-season villa pricing is about half of June — a 3BR oceanfront in Sea Pines runs around $400–500/night in February vs $1,000+ in June.

Mid-60s most days, occasional 70s. Golf is playable — Atlantic Dunes, Heron Point, Harbour Town all open. Some beachfront restaurants close mid-week but the year-rounders (Skull Creek Boathouse, Old Oyster Factory, Lucky Rooster, Hudson's on the Docks) stay open.

For four nights, you can do the flight + 3BR villa + a round of golf + three dinners for under $5K for a couple. Cheaper than Sandals.

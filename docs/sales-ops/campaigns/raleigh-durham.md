# Raleigh-Durham → Hilton Head Campaign Brief

## 1. Audience profile
- **Top 3 zip codes by HHI/travel propensity**
  - 27517 (Chapel Hill/Meadowmont/Governors Club) — median HHI ~$165K
  - 27613 (Northwest Raleigh/North Hills corridor) — median HHI ~$155K
  - 27519 (Cary/Preston) — median HHI ~$170K
- **Income / household composition:** Research Triangle Park technology and pharma (IBM, Cisco, MetLife, Fidelity, GSK, Biogen RTP), Duke and UNC Health systems, Duke and UNC faculty cohort. Families with kids in Cary Academy, Ravenscroft, Durham Academy.
- **Drive-time vs flight math:** Raleigh to HHI is 5h via I-40 W → I-95 S → I-26 E → US-278, or 5h15m via US-1 → I-95 S. American flies RDU → SAV (~1h05m direct, 2x daily peak). RDU is a clean drive market — most families drive.
- **Feeder communities:** The Country Club at Wakefield Plantation, Carolina Country Club (Raleigh), Hope Valley CC (Durham), Governors Club (Chapel Hill); Duke alumni Triangle chapter; UNC alumni — both are HHI-affinity networks.
- **Peak booking month for HHI:** February (booking summer + spring break), September. Wake County public schools spring break is typically the first week of April — a week before DC, two before Boston, so RDU catches good inventory.

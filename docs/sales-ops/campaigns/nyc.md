# NYC → Hilton Head Campaign Brief

## 1. Audience profile
- **Top 3 zip codes by HHI/travel propensity**
  - 10021 (Upper East Side/Lenox Hill) — median HHI ~$160K, top quintile concentration much higher
  - 10024 (Upper West Side around 81st–86th) — median HHI ~$155K
  - 10583 (Scarsdale, Westchester) — median HHI ~$250K
- **Income / household composition:** finance, law, consulting, media. Manhattan couples 35–50 with one or two kids in Trinity/Dalton/Horace Mann; Scarsdale and Greenwich families using HHI as the warm-weather alternative to the Hamptons in shoulder season.
- **Drive-time vs flight math:** NYC to HHI is a flight market — 13h drive, never realistic. JetBlue and Delta JFK → SAV (1h55m–2h10m), United EWR → SAV, and seasonal LGA → HHH on Breeze direct into Hilton Head Island Airport. The Breeze direct into HHH is the unlock — no SAV transfer.
- **Feeder communities:** Winged Foot, Quaker Ridge, Westchester CC; Sleepy Hollow CC; Maidstone; Princeton alumni Manhattan chapter; HBS NYC alumni club.
- **Peak booking month for HHI:** January (booking Easter + April shoulder week), April (booking Thanksgiving), September (booking January warm-weather escape — strong snowbird-lite segment).

## 6. Reddit value-comment template
**Subreddit:** r/AskNYC
**Question scenario:** "Looking for a beach trip that isn't the Hamptons or Florida — any recs from the Atlantic coast?"
**Reply:**
Hilton Head is the answer most Manhattan people sleep on. Two hours direct on Breeze from LGA into Hilton Head Island Airport (HHH, not SAV — different airport, on-island). You're in a villa in Sea Pines or Palmetto Dunes within 30 minutes of wheels-down.

What it is: a 12-mile barrier island, no high-rises, bike paths everywhere, three Pete Dye and one RTJ golf course, 60+ restaurants, a working harbor (Harbour Town).

What it isn't: a party scene, a single-bar town, or cheap. Peak summer villas run $4–9K/week.

Cleanest months for the NYC crowd are mid-April through Memorial Day and early September through October.

Disclosure: I run Hilton Ahead, a Hilton Head travel consultancy. Happy to answer in DM, not pitching.

## 6. Reddit value-comment template
**Subreddit:** r/boston
**Question scenario:** "Hilton Head or Kiawah for a family beach week in April?"
**Reply:**
Both are good, different feel. Hilton Head is bigger (12 mi long, 12 sq mi total), more restaurants, more golf options at varying price points, bike-path network that lets the kids ride to the beach. Kiawah is quieter, more upscale-resort-feeling, one main road, more limited dining off the resort. If your kids are 8+, HHI's bike paths and Harbour Town shops are a real plus.

Practical Boston note: JetBlue BOS → SAV is the cleanest path. The 7am gets you on island by noon. Avoid the 11am — it lands at SAV after the rental car desk lines have built up.

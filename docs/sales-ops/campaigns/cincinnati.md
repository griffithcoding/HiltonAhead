# Cincinnati → Hilton Head Campaign Brief

## 1. Audience profile
- **Top 3 zip codes by HHI/travel propensity**
  - 45243 (Indian Hill) — median HHI ~$200K, highest in OH
  - 45208 (Hyde Park) — median HHI ~$130K, high concentration of dual-income professionals
  - 41017 (Crestview Hills, KY — Greater Cincinnati south) — median HHI ~$140K
- **Income / household composition:** P&G corporate, Cintas, Kroger HQ, Western & Southern, Fifth Third leadership. Indian Hill is one of the wealthiest zips in the Midwest. Families with kids in Cincinnati Country Day, Seven Hills, Summit Country Day.
- **Drive-time vs flight math:** Cincinnati to HHI is 10h via I-75 S → I-26 E → I-95 S, doable in one long day. Delta and American fly CVG → SAV (1h55m direct, fewer daily flights — usually 2x daily peak). Many Cincinnati families drive given the gear load.
- **Feeder communities:** Cincinnati Country Club, Camargo Club, Kenwood CC, Maketewah CC; Miami University (Oxford) alumni — particularly the Miami → HHI summer pipeline is well-established; Xavier alumni; UC Lindner business school network.
- **Peak booking month for HHI:** February–March (booking summer), September (booking Thanksgiving + spring break). Cincinnati public/private school spring break is typically late March — earlier than DC/Boston, so Cincinnati families catch shoulder-season inventory.

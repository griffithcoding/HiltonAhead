# Greenville-SC → Hilton Head Campaign Brief

## 1. Audience profile
- **Top 3 zip codes by HHI/travel propensity**
  - 29609 (North Main/Paris Mountain) — median HHI ~$150K, growing fast
  - 29615 (Eastside/Greenville Country Club corridor) — median HHI ~$135K
  - 29687 (Travelers Rest/Cliffs Valley corridor) — median HHI ~$140K, high concentration of recent transplants and second-home owners
- **Income / household composition:** Michelin North America HQ, Bosch corporate, BMW Spartanburg leadership, large healthcare (Prisma, Bon Secours), private-equity-backed manufacturing leadership. Substantial cohort of Northeast and Midwest transplants in the Cliffs communities. Families with kids in Christ Church Episcopal, Greenville High, Eastside.
- **Drive-time vs flight math:** Greenville to HHI is 4h15m via I-26 E → US-278 — the cleanest single-highway drive in the Southeast. GSP → SAV exists but is connection-only; nobody flies this route. Pure drive market.
- **Feeder communities:** Greenville Country Club, Thornblade Club, The Cliffs at Glassy/Valley/Mountain Park; Furman alumni; Clemson alumni Greenville chapter; Greenville Tech professional network.
- **Peak booking month for HHI:** February (booking summer), September. Greenville County School District's spring break is typically the first week of April; many private schools take the second week of April. Greenville families also disproportionately do HHI long weekends in May and September due to drive proximity.

# Travel-Agent Commission Plan — Collecting ~10% on Bookings You Already Make

**Date:** 2026-06-02
**Owner:** William Griffith
**Why this exists:** Hilton Ahead books Hilton Head lodging by hand for clients. Without travel-agent accreditation, those bookings earn **$0 commission** — the property keeps the agent share. With accreditation, the same bookings pay **8–10%**. This is higher-margin than any affiliate program and more on-brand for a local consultancy.

> **Verified 2026-06-02** via IATA, Marriott, Vacasa, Fora, HostAgencyReviews. Web-sourced; confirm supplier specifics in each portal before relying on a number.

## The core decision: TIDS (free, DIY) vs Host Agency (split, full access)

You need a recognized accreditation **number** to be paid commission. Three real options for a small independent consultancy (full IATA/IATAN self-accreditation requires sales-volume proof + bond + months — skip for now):

| Path | Cost | You keep | Access | Admin | Best when |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **TIDS** (Travel Industry Designator Service) | **Free** | ~100% of commission | Suppliers that accept TIDS (Marriott ✅, Vacasa ✅, many hotels) | You register + invoice + track each supplier yourself | You want max retention and will self-administer |
| **Host agency** (e.g. Fora) | ~$300–700/yr + **30% split** (Fora 70/30 → 80/20 over $300k, no minimums) | 70–80% | Their IATA # + consortia (Virtuoso), preferred/VIP rates, central commission tracking, training | They handle tracking/payment | You want luxury/consortia perks + less admin + credibility |
| **Full IATA / IATAN** | Sales minimums + bond, months | ~100% | Gold standard, everything | Heavy | Later, at scale |

**Key facts (verified):**
- **TIDS is free** and is an accepted commission ID for non-IATA intermediaries (advisors/consultants). It does NOT grant airline ticketing (BSP) — irrelevant for you; you book lodging + golf, not flights.
- **Marriott** pays **10%** to Preferred agencies, **8%** to standard — and explicitly accepts **ARC / IATA / IATAN / TIDS / CLIA** as the accreditation number. So TIDS earns you 8–10% on the HHI MVC villas + the Westin today.
- **Hilton** pays up to **10%** to IATA-registered agencies. ⚠️ **VERIFY** whether Hilton accepts a bare TIDS code or requires full IATA — confirm in the Hilton agent portal before assuming. (Covers the HHI Hilton properties + Ocean Oak HGV.)
- **Vacasa** pays **8%** of base rental (excl. fees/tax) via its Travel Advisor Program — and Vacasa manages a chunk of **Sea Pines + Palmetto Dunes** villa inventory. This is the practical path to villa commission. Book via Vacasa.com or the MyBookingPal advisor portal.
- **Fora** (modern host): 70/30 split, **no sales minimums/quotas**, includes Virtuoso + Four Seasons Preferred + Rosewood Elite. Traditional hosts run 50/50→90/10, sometimes with quotas.

## Recommended path: TIDS now, evaluate a host agency in 30–60 days

**Sequence to capture money fastest while keeping options open:**

### Phase 1 — This week (free, immediate)
1. **Register for a free TIDS code** at iata.org → Travel Agency Program → TIDS. Business: Hilton Ahead Travel Co. Takes minutes to apply.
2. **Enroll in supplier agent programs** using the TIDS code:
   - **Marriott** — travelagents.marriott.com → register agency → enter TIDS. Earns 8–10% on MVC villas + Westin. (Pursue "Preferred" status criteria to reach 10%.)
   - **Vacasa** — vacasa.com/travel-agent-partner-program → submit credentials. 8% on Sea Pines/Palmetto Dunes villas they manage.
   - **Hilton** — hilton.com/en/portal/travel-agents → register. ⚠️ confirm TIDS is accepted vs full IATA.
3. **Start booking client lodging under the TIDS code.** Every commissionable rate now pays you. Money starts on the next consumed stay (commissions pay *after* checkout).

### Phase 2 — Weeks 2–8 (decide on a host)
4. **Pilot a host agency** if you want: Virtuoso/STARS-Luminous **VIP rates** (upgrades, resort credits, breakfast — a real differentiator you can sell), centralized commission collection, and access to suppliers that gate behind a consortium. **Fora** fits a content-driven local advisor (no minimums, 70/30). Traditional hosts (Gateway, Travel Planners Intl, KHM) offer higher splits but may attach quotas.
5. **Compare net economics** on your real volume (see math below) before committing — the host's 30% cut vs. the perks + admin relief.

### Phase 3 — Direct resort deals (the local edge)
6. **Call Sea Pines Resort and Palmetto Dunes** property/group sales directly. Ask their travel-advisor commission terms for direct (non-Vacasa) bookings. These are relationship deals — a 30-year local consultancy is exactly who they say yes to. ⚠️ Terms unpublished; get them in writing.

Assume you hand-book **$200,000/yr** in commissionable HHI lodging (villas + resorts) at a ~9% blended rate:

| Path | Gross commission | Your net |
|---|---|---|
| No accreditation (today) | $0 | **$0** |

## TIDS Application Walkthrough (verified 2026-06-02)

**Where:** [iata.org/en/services/travel-agency-program/tids/application](https://www.iata.org/en/services/travel-agency-program/tids/application/)
**Cost:** Free (application + renewal fees eliminated). **Processing:** 3–5 business days → approval email with your TIDS code.

### The 4 steps
1. **Assemble documents** (below) before you start — the form wants uploads.
2. **Register / log in** to the IATA Customer Portal (create an account if new).
3. Go to **Services → select TIDS**.
4. **Complete + submit** the application with uploads.

### Documents required
| Doc | What it means for Hilton Ahead | Status to check |
|---|---|---|
| Legal form of business + ownership | LLC formation docs or registered sole-prop / DBA for "Hilton Ahead Travel Co." | ⚠️ Confirm the entity is registered with SC |
| Business + tax registration number | EIN and/or SC business registration number | ⚠️ Have EIN ready |
| Bank letter or statement in the **business** name | A business bank account statement/letter | ⚠️ Needs a business account (not personal) |
| **1 letter of recommendation** from an IATA airline, a GDS (Amadeus/Sabre/Worldspan), **or a major travel supplier (hotel, car rental)** | A Hilton Head hotel/resort/DMC contact can write this | 🎯 See gotcha below |
| Gov-issued seller-of-travel license *(where applicable)* | **Not applicable in SC** — skip | ✅ N/A |

### ⚠️ The one gotcha: the recommendation letter
This is the chicken-and-egg snag for brand-new advisors — but it's an **advantage for you.** A letter from any hotel, resort, DMC, or car-rental supplier counts, and you have a 30-year Hilton Head hospitality network. One email to a resort GM, golf director, or villa-management contact you already know gets you a compliant letter. (If you'd rather skip it entirely, a **host agency** removes the rec-letter requirement — they're already accredited.)

### Before you apply — 3 prerequisites to line up
1. **Registered business entity** — "Hilton Ahead Travel Co." as an SC LLC or registered DBA. If it's not formally registered, do that first (SC Secretary of State).
2. **EIN** — free from IRS.gov, instant.
3. **Business bank account** — for the bank letter. Open one if revenue currently flows through personal.

Then: get the recommendation letter → upload everything → submit. Code in ~a week → enter it in the Marriott / Vacasa / Hilton agent portals → start collecting.

## DECISION: Host agency = Fora (chosen 2026-06-02)

Going the **host-agency** route (not DIY TIDS) — trades ~30% of commission for instant accreditation, consortia/VIP rates, central commission collection, and no rec-letter requirement. Picked **Fora** for this profile.

### Why Fora for Hilton Ahead specifically
- **Keeps the Hilton Ahead brand** client-facing (verified — many traditional hosts force their own brand).
- **Built for content/SEO-driven advisors** — matches a site engineered for organic + LLM citation.
- **No minimums / no quotas** — formalizing commission on already-hand-booked trips without pressure.
- **Removes the TIDS rec-letter / book-of-business barrier** — fast onboarding.
- **Virtuoso + Four Seasons Preferred + Rosewood Elite from day one** — VIP upgrades/credits to sell as a differentiator, plus higher base commissions.
- **$299/yr** (or $99/qtr). Split: **70/30 → 80/20 over $300k sales.**

### The tradeoff (and when to revisit)
Fora's split caps at 70–80%. High-split IC hosts (Travel Planners Intl, Gateway, OASIS, KHM) reach **90/10** but with higher fees, less tech, and they favor high-volume back-office-only producers. Note: **70% of a Fora preferred rate can beat 90% of a standard rate** — base commission matters as much as split. Revisit only if volume gets large enough that the 10–20% split delta outweighs Fora's perks + ease.

### Application steps
1. Apply at **foratravel.com/join** (also `/pricing` for plan, `/apply`).
2. Pick plan: **$299/yr** or $99/qtr.
3. Complete Fora's **onboarding training** (their gated course to unlock booking).
4. Receive access to Fora's **IATA number + booking platform (Fora Portal)**.
5. Start booking HHI lodging through Fora → commission tracked + paid centrally.
6. Set client-facing brand to **Hilton Ahead** in the Fora profile.

### Supplier coverage to confirm during onboarding
- **Marriott** (MVC villas + Westin) — preferred/STARS access via Fora's consortia.
- **Hilton** (HHI properties + Ocean Oak HGV).
- **Vacasa** villas (Sea Pines / Palmetto Dunes inventory) — confirm Fora's villa-booking path.
- **Direct Sea Pines / Palmetto Dunes resort rates** — ask Fora supplier-relations whether these are bookable through their programs; if not, pursue direct (separate from host).

> Once live, the optional CRM hook (commissionable-booking tracking) becomes worth building — flag hand-booked trips with commission owed.
